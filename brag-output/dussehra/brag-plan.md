# Market Movers Dussehra reel (brush only)

- Format: 1080x1920 (9:16), 13s, silent until a licensed track is added.
- Look: every visual is painted live by p5.brush 2.2.3 (MIT) on one WEBGL canvas, frame by frame, with fixed seeds so every render is identical. Strokes "boil" (re-roll their texture 8 times a second) like hand-drawn animation. Only the type and the official MM mark are not painted.
- Colour rule learned on the Navratri reel: p5.brush mixes pigment against white paper, so dark or saturated colours are laid down as `brush.wash` shapes, and brush strokes and watercolour fills add texture on top.

## Beats
1. 0 to 1.6s: paper fills with a sunset (wash rings, watercolour texture, dry-brush cloud streaks), the sun pops and its rays are painted in; the ground is brushed in from the left. "शुभ दशहरा / Happy Dussehra" at 1.0s.
2. 1.3 to 2.9s: the Ravana effigy is painted up from the ground: body, gold sash, arms, sword, then ten crowned heads from the centre out.
3. 3.9 to 4.5s: an arrow flies in from the bottom left and hits the chest; a paint burst.
4. 4.55 to 8.0s: three layers of flame rise and swallow the effigy, which sinks into the ground; embers and charcoal smoke curls. "बुराई पर अच्छाई की जीत / Good wins over evil" at 5.9s.
5. 8.0s: torn-paper wipe (marigold over teal) into the end card.
6. 8.3 to 13s: teal watercolour end card, MM mark, Market Movers, a painted arrow flies under the name, "विजयादशमी की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ", Marhatal, Jabalpur · marketmovers.co.in.
