# Brag Plan: Market Movers

## What is this app?
The catalogue site for Market Movers, a wholesale plumbing and sanitary distributor in Marhatal, Jabalpur: ten brands of pipes, fittings and sanitaryware from stock, ordered by sending a list on WhatsApp.

## The angle
A calm, confident trade film, not a startup ad. The strength of the business is physical and concrete: real stock, real pipe sizes, real brands, and the simplest possible way to order. The video shows that strength through the site's own visuals (the woven MM logo, the pipe photos behind frosted glass, the IS 4985 size rings, the count-up figures) and connects them with match cuts so the whole thing flows as one move: logo to header, pipe end to size rings, outer ring to HDPE coil, list to WhatsApp, header back to logo.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The two Ms of the woven logo slide in from above and below and lock together on the beat, then "Market Movers" wipes out beside them, full width. It is the site's own load animation, scaled up for video.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The pipe stack photo with "Pipes, fittings and sanitaryware, from stock." on frosted glass. The camera then zooms into one pipe opening and that opening becomes the size rings.
- The IS 4985 size rings draw in to scale; the selected wall sweeps out from 20 mm to 400 mm while the number rolls and the use changes ("House plumbing" to "Large mains and sewerage").
- The 400 mm ring opens into the HDPE coil photo: "Ready stock, so your site never waits on a factory." with the four figures counting up: 1,500+ products, 10 brands, 44 product lines, 7 product families.
- The ten brands slide in one by one; the five with full pages get the site's teal marker.

## Outro / punchline
"Send the list. We'll handle the rest." A list is sent on WhatsApp and a reply comes back, then the header logo grows back to centre with a teal "WhatsApp your list" pill, the phone number and marketmovers.co.in.

## User flow worth showing
Send your list on WhatsApp → get rates the same day → pick up or we deliver. Shown as a phone chat (illustrative list of real product types from the catalogue, no prices) beside the site's three ordering steps.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, premium trade film; smooth continuous flow
- Interpretation: fewer, longer holds; soft transitions built as match cuts and iris reveals; no hard cuts, no shouting, one teal accent.

## Format: landscape, 1920x1080, 30fps
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f4f4f2 (off-white), card #fafaf9
- Accent: #0b5d63 (teal)
- Text: #111111, muted #62625e
- Display font: Geist (variable), 600, tight tracking (-0.045em)
- Body font: Geist; Hindi lines in IBM Plex Sans Devanagari
- Strongest visual element: generated product photos inside 24px rounded frames with frosted glass panels over them; the woven MM logo; the to-scale size rings.

## Share copy (draft)
Ten brands of pipes, fittings and sanitaryware, in stock in Marhatal, Jabalpur. Send your list on WhatsApp and get rates the same day: marketmovers.co.in

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady, clean; about 110 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, volume around 0.35, fades out over the last 1.5s so the final accent rings over it
- Music cue guidance: preset read (`assets/music/cues/...vol-12...json`). Strong cues: 8.74s (size rings start sweeping), 22.93s (logo lands in outro). Beat grid ~0.545s; stats on 12.02 / 12.55 / 13.11 / 13.64; brands accent every other beat.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; a soft teal glow behind the photo frames breathes with music RMS. No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: logo lock, glass panel arrival, size sweep, stats count, keyboard taps while typing the list, message send and reply, final logo landing.
- Restraint rule: no bright or repeated clicky sounds; SFX sit under the music.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Logo lock — 0 to 3.3s
Off-white field. Inner M drops, outer M rises, they lock. "Market Movers" wipes in beside the mark, with "मार्केट मूवर्स" underneath. Then the whole lockup shrinks into a floating glass header pill at top-left while the pipe-stack photo frame rises from below.
Sequential/interaction: yes, the two Ms arrive one after the other.
Audio intent: confident start. Audio-coupled idea: soft impact as the Ms lock.
Transition mood: soft, continuous (logo becomes the header) → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — From stock — 3.3 to 7.6s
Pipe stack photo in a rounded frame, slow push. Glass panel slides up: label "Wholesale distributor in Marhatal, Jabalpur", headline "Pipes, fittings and sanitaryware, from stock." (tail in muted), teal "WhatsApp your list" pill. Headline holds about 2.5s settled.
Sequential/interaction: label, headline words, button arrive in order.
Audio intent: settle in. Audio-coupled idea: soft drop on the panel.
Transition mood: zoom into one pipe opening; an iris opens from that opening → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Every size — 7.6 to 11s
Off-white. Left: "From house plumbing to 400 mm mains." Right: the 15 concentric rings (20 to 400 mm, to scale) draw in, then the selected wall and teal dimension line sweep outward while the mm number rolls 20 → 400 and the use line changes. Size ruler below with the active size bold. Caption: "Outside diameters to scale, IS 4985 series."
Sequential/interaction: rings draw in one by one; sweep simulates picking sizes.
Audio intent: lift. Audio-coupled idea: sweep starts on the 8.74s strong cue.
Transition mood: the 400 mm ring becomes an iris that opens on the coil photo → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Ready stock — 11 to 15.3s
HDPE coil photo frame. Glass panel left: "Ready stock, so your site never waits on a factory." Glass panel right: four figures roll up on the beat: 1,500+ products in stock at the godown; 10 brands on our shelves; 44 product lines with full specifications online; 7 product families.
Sequential/interaction: four figures, one per beat, then hold together.
Audio intent: confidence. Audio-coupled idea: one soft accent on the first figure only.
Transition mood: frame slides up and away, next scene rises behind it → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Brands we stock — 15.3 to 18.6s
Off-white. "Brands we stock" with "हमारे ब्रांड" below. Ten brand names (Finolex, Dutron, Texmo, Waterflo, Johnson Bathrooms, Kisan, Plasto, MBH, Rewa, Reco) slide up one by one in a large flowing index; the five with pages get the teal marker. Line: "Ten brands in stock at our Marhatal counter."
Sequential/interaction: yes, names one by one (texture), then hold the full set.
Audio intent: momentum. Audio-coupled idea: one soft slide sound as the list starts.
Transition mood: wipe up into the counter photo → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Send the list — 18.6 to 22.9s
Counter photo frame. Glass panel left: "Send the list. We'll handle the rest." (words darken one by one, as on the site), then the three steps: Send your list; Get rates the same day; Pick up or we deliver. Right: a phone with a WhatsApp chat to Market Movers. The list types itself ("110 mm SWR pipe x 20", "1 inch CPVC elbow x 50", "Solvent cement x 4"), is sent, and a reply arrives: "Got it. Rates coming your way today."
Sequential/interaction: typing, send, reply; steps highlight in step with the chat.
Audio intent: the product in use. Audio-coupled idea: quiet key taps, a soft send click and a reply drop.
Transition mood: frame scales away; the header pill grows back to centre → Scene 7

### Scene 7 — Outro — 22.9 to 25s
Off-white. The woven logo and "Market Movers" land in the centre on the 22.93s strong cue. Below: teal "WhatsApp your list" pill, "+91 94250 66923", "marketmovers.co.in", "4 & 5 Anjuman Market, Marhatal, Jabalpur".
Audio intent: resolve. Audio-coupled idea: bell on the landing, music fades under it.

**Music mood for this video:** polished, warm, steady
**Audio summary:** a steady, clean bed from the first frame with a handful of soft, motion-matched accents, resolving on a bell as the logo lands and the music fades.

Note: the chat list is illustrative UI text (product types from the catalogue, no prices, no customer names). All other copy and figures come from the site (`src/pages/index.astro`, `src/data/site.ts`, `src/data/brands/*.json`).
