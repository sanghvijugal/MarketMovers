# Diwali reel: image prompts for Gemini

Paste the **style line** at the start of every prompt so the set matches. Generate at the highest resolution Gemini offers,
download as PNG, keep the file names below and upload them here. All text in the video is typeset in code, so every prompt
asks for no lettering (except the optional headline lettering).

**Style line (prepend to every prompt):**
> Indian truck-art and Bollywood kitsch illustration, hand-painted look with bold black outlines, flat saturated colours
> (hot pink, cyan, marigold yellow, emerald green, royal blue, orange), lotus and paisley motifs, playful and festive, Diwali mood.

**Stickers** are generated on a plain mid-grey background with a thick white die-cut border so I can cut them out cleanly:
> Single die-cut sticker, centred, with a thick white sticker border, on a plain flat solid mid-grey (#808080) background,
> no shadow, no text, no other objects.
